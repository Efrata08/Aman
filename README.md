# Aman — Your Immigration Co-Pilot 

> *Aman* (أمان / አማን) means **safety and peace** in Urdu and Amharic. For 45 million immigrants navigating the US immigration system alone, that's exactly what's missing.

---

## The Problem

Every year, thousands of immigrants lose their legal status in America — not because they did anything wrong, but because they missed a deadline they didn't know about, made a paperwork error they couldn't catch, or didn't hear about a policy change that affected their case. Immigration attorneys charge $300/hour. Most immigrants can't afford that.

---

## What Aman Does

Aman is an AI-powered immigration co-pilot that protects your legal status in America.

### Core Features

**📊 Personalized Status Dashboard**
Upload your immigration documents and Aman builds a dashboard tailored to your specific case — visa type, filing history, current status, and upcoming actions.

**⏰ Deadline Prediction Engine**
Aman predicts your critical deadlines based on your specific case type and real USCIS processing times — not generic estimates. Get notified before it's too late.

**📋 Document Checklist**
Before you apply for anything — a visa extension, EAD, green card, OPT — Aman tells you exactly which documents you need and flags what's missing from your package before you file.

**🔍 Pre-Filing Document Review**
Upload your complete document package before submission. Aman reviews it for errors, inconsistencies, and common red flags that trigger denials or removal proceedings.

**📡 24/7 Policy Monitoring**
Immigration policy changes constantly. Aman monitors USCIS updates around the clock and alerts you the moment a change affects your specific situation.

**🌍 Multilingual Support**
Built for immigrant communities. Aman supports:
- English
- Spanish
- Amharic (አማርኛ)
- Urdu (اردو)
- Arabic (العربية)
- Hindi (हिन्दी)
- More coming soon

## Business Model

$15/month subscription — direct B2C. No middleman, no attorney markups.

For immigrants, this is a **painkiller, not a vitamin**. The cost of getting it wrong (deportation, loss of work authorization, visa denial) vastly outweighs a $15/month subscription. Word-of-mouth in tight-knit immigrant communities drives organic growth.
